Allan Clark

With 23 years’ experience, well over 700 homes successfully sold, and more than 50,000 hours ‘on the job’ you can be confident the sale of your home is in good hands

We like to think Real Estate is a straightforward process and occasionally it is. But every property comes with a set of dynamics that make each sale unique. There is the property itself, house style, compliance issues (or not), presentation requirements, locality, market conditions, budgets, buyer trends and timing issues. These are part and parcel of every sale process and over the last two decades I have worked with pretty much every combination of the above that you can think of.

But what makes each sale different is the people. Their needs, wants, desires, plans, lifestyles, motivations, expectations, capabilities and resources. And when you add in personalities, personal history, their experience and take on life, every sale becomes distinctly unique.

Handling your unique equation is what brings my job to life and it’s a job I love and understand well.
