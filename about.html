A bit about me

Meet Allan.

As agents we’re often invited behind the scenes of our clients’ lives. I think it’s only fair that I show you a little of mine.

Two careers so far: rock & roll and real estate.

Before real estate, my world was music. I worked as a professional musician and ran a record company, learning the fundamentals of marketing, storytelling and building an audience.

In 2001 I moved to Auckland and changed direction. Real estate became the second major chapter of my working life.

More than two decades later, I’ve sold over 700 homes and spent more than 15,000 hours doing the work. Along the way I’ve learned that while every property is different, people generally want the same thing: good information, straight answers and someone prepared to genuinely care about the outcome.
